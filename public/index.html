    How it works

    the DSA behind the demo

    visit(url)
    Inserts a new node right after current, then drops everything ahead of it — just like a real browser erases forward history once you navigate somewhere new. O(1)
    back(steps)
    Walks current toward head along prev pointers, stopping at the oldest page. O(steps)
    forward(steps)
    Walks current toward the newest page along next pointers. O(steps)
    space
    One node per visited page for the lifetime of the tab. O(n)
